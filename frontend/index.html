About the System

Our platform enables secure remote voting for eligible voters. Identity verification is performed during registration; your ballot remains anonymous and end‑to‑end encrypted. After polls close, verifiable tallies and public audit proofs ensure transparency without compromising voter privacy.

How it works

1. Register & verify

Sign up and confirm identity via OTP / ID check.

2. View ballot

Access your eligible elections and candidates.

3. Cast vote

Your encrypted ballot is submitted securely.

4. Get confirmation

Receive a receipt ID to verify inclusion in the count.

Frequently asked questions

How is my vote kept anonymous?

We separate identity verification from ballot casting and use cryptography so submitted votes cannot be linked to accounts.

What devices are supported?

Modern browsers on desktop and mobile with updated operating systems. No app install required.

Can I verify my vote?

Yes—after submitting, you receive a receipt ID that appears in the public audit list once tallied.
